# The Paradigm Shift: From Harness to Saddle

Artificial intelligence moves at a blinding speed. What we consider "traditional" today was bleeding-edge barely a year or two ago. In that traditional era, the entire industry obsessed over one question: *How do we make the model do the work?*

The answer they arrived at was the **harness**.

Frameworks like Codex, Claude Code, and early agentic wrappers were designed exactly like a horse’s harness. A harness is utilitarian and rigid. It straps across the entire body of the animal, locking it into a specific posture so it can pull a heavy, pre-determined cart along a single, straight track. You build a coding harness to pull the coding cart; you build a research harness to pull the research cart. The model is powerful, but it is restricted by the rigidity of the straps you place on it.

But true autonomy doesn't come from pulling a cart. It comes from agility. It comes from a symbiotic connection between human intent and raw power, capable of changing direction in a millisecond. If you want to unlock the true potential of autonomous infrastructure, you have to **"Trade your harness for a saddle."**

### 24/7 Autonomous Infrastructure: The End of Reinventing the Wheel

Agents aren't chatbots anymore; they are **autonomous infrastructure**. You can have persistent agents running 24/7 on dedicated cloud servers, watching financial markets, analyzing news, auditing codebases, synthesizing reports, and executing multi-step business logic independently while you sleep.

Historically, to build a 24/7 agent on a VPS, you had to reinvent the wheel every single time: write custom Python loops, manually set up systemd cron jobs, stitch together vector databases, and build a rigid harness just to make that specific agent pull its cart.

**Saddle is the universal foundation for 24/7 autonomous agents at scale.**

Instead of reinventing the wheel for every new VPS you spin up, you simply install Saddle:
* **Saddle Lite:** A single-user, zero-overhead edge engine for running private 24/7 background tasks on a cheap $4/mo VPS.
* **Saddle Cloud:** An enterprise-scale, multi-tenant fleet of agents billing users via Stripe and scaling across containers.

The background task dock inside Saddle is the foundation that turns bare metal servers into self-operating digital workhorses.
